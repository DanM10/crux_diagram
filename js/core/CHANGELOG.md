* `computeGeometry(document, { measurer, style? })`: paint-ready shapes and joints in canvas units, for painters outside the browser (spec 06).
* `validateGeometry(document, geometry)`: checks the shape of a geometry, with stable error codes.
* `LABEL_FONT_FAMILY` moves here from `@crux-diagram/react`.
* `JointPlacement` gains `textSize`.
